Privacy Policy

Gulf Coast Contest Club · Last updated: August 27, 2026

Gulf Coast Contest Club ("GCCC," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect from our members and how we use and protect it.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information a member provides to us directly, which may include name, amateur radio call sign, email address, and mobile phone number. We collect this information when you join the club, sign up on our membership form or roster, or contact us.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to operate the club and communicate with our members — for example, to send announcements about amateur radio contests and special events, on-air operating schedules, meeting reminders, and event coordination. We do not use your information for any commercial or marketing purpose unrelated to the club.

Text Messaging (SMS)

If you provide your mobile number and consent, we may send you text message notifications about club activities and events. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, and you can reply HELP for assistance.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties. Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in information are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. Information is kept within the club and used only for the purposes described above.

Data Retention

We retain member information only for as long as you are a member or as needed to communicate with you. You may ask us to remove your information at any time.

Your Choices

You may update or request deletion of your information, or opt out of text messages (reply STOP), at any time by contacting us.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Email us at gulfcoastcontestclub@gmail.com.
