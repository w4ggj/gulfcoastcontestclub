Contest calendar

Major HF contests for GCCC members. Submit scores under the NE2CC club affiliation to count toward club standings.

Dates shift slightly year to year. Verify exact dates at arrl.org/contest-calendar and cqww.com. All members are encouraged to submit claimed scores to 3830scores.com under the NE2CC club affiliation.

Score submission

Submit all claimed scores to 3830scores.com and list NE2CC as your club affiliation. This counts toward club aggregate standings in ARRL and CQ contests.

Logging software

Club members use N1MM+, Win-Test, and N3FJP depending on the contest. Reach out via the if you need help getting set up with contest logging.

Field Day

ARRL Field Day is our biggest club event of the year. We operate as a group from a local site — members only. Watch News for site announcements.
