Program Description

Gulf Coast Contest Club sends text message notifications to members about club activities — including amateur radio contest and special-event operating times, on-air schedules, meeting reminders, and event coordination.

How to Opt In

You opt in to receive text messages by providing your mobile number to the club and agreeing to receive club text notifications on our membership signup form or roster. Only members who have joined and opted in receive messages.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on club activity and events.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier plan. Gulf Coast Contest Club does not charge for these messages.

How to Opt Out

You can cancel the text message service at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us. After you send STOP, we will send a message to confirm that you have been unsubscribed and will stop sending you text messages.

Help

If you are experiencing any issues, reply with the keyword HELP for more assistance, or email us at gulfcoastcontestclub@gmail.com.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

For information on how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy. We do not sell or share your mobile number or SMS opt-in information with any third parties.
